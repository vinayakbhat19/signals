Signals

COMING SOONFind your people. Build, play, team up.

Signals is our campus app. Post a short Signal saying what you are looking for, and people nearby on campus answer with "I'm in".

  • Need a teammateHackathon, contest or project: find the missing person for your team.
  • Want to buildGot an idea? Find a developer, designer or someone to build it with.
  • Open to connectSay hi to people with the same interests, at a talk or in the hostel.
  • Open to workOffer your skills for a club, a fest team or a startup.
How it works 1. Post a Signal in 20 seconds. 2. People tap "I'm in". 3. You accept the ones you like. Contact details are shared only after you accept, and Signals expire by themselves.
