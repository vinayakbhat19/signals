Add Fest Live to your Home Screen

It gets its own icon and opens full screen like an app. Free, under 1 MB, and it works on weak campus signal.

  1. 1 Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari
  2. 2 Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. 3 Tap Add, then open Fest Live from your Home Screen
Android: tap the Add to Home Screen button below, or Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
