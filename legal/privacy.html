Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: Signals only asks for what it needs: your name, your email and what you do in the app. We never ask for your phone number, location, contacts or ID. We do not sell your data. You can delete your account any time.

1. Who we are

Signals is a campus app built and run by Vinayak Bhat ("we", "us"). Fiesta'26 Live is a temporary mode of Signals for the Fiesta 2026 college fest (25 to 27 November 2026). This policy covers both. Contact: vinayakkbhatt@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

WhatWhy
Your name and email, and your password (stored only as a one-way scrambled value; we never see it)To create your account and sign you in
If you choose Google sign-in: your Google name, email and profile pictureThe same, without a password
Optional: your departmentShown only if you add it
Your votes (private), your treasure-hunt stamps and the time you scanned them, your "I'm in" replies and postsTo run voting, the hunt, the leaderboard and Signals
Your device's alert address, if you turn on alertsTo send you alerts such as a delayed event
Basic technical logs kept by our hosting providers (for example your IP address and browser type)To keep the service running and secure

We do not collect your phone number, exact location, contacts, photos from your phone, or any ID number. Camera access is used only to scan QR codes on your device; the camera image is not saved or sent anywhere.

3. Who can see what

  • Other students see your first name and the first letter of your last name on the treasure-hunt leaderboard (you can hide yourself in My account). They never see who you voted for.
  • Volunteers and organisers can see the names and emails of the top hunters, only to contact prize winners.
  • Event hosts see the people registered for their own event, using the lists they get from their own registration forms.
  • In Signals, your contact details are shared with another person only after you accept them.

4. Who handles your data for us

  • Supabase: database and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: hosting the app, sending alerts, and cookie-free visit statistics.
  • An email service (Brevo or Gmail): sends the 6-digit sign-up and password codes.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".

These providers process data to run the service. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

5. Cookies and storage

Signals sets no cookies. It keeps your sign-in and a few settings (like dismissed banners) in your phone's own storage so you stay signed in.

6. How long we keep it

  • After the fest, individual votes, alert addresses and participants' contact details are deleted. Winners and totals may stay as a record of the fest.
  • Your account stays until you delete it. Use Delete my account in My account to remove your account and its data.

7. Your choices

  • Hide yourself from the leaderboard, switch off alerts, or delete your account in My account.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data: vinayakkbhatt@gmail.com.

8. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, access is limited by role, and important actions are logged. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

9. Who can use Signals

Signals is meant for college students. If you are under 18, please use it only with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is important.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: vinayakkbhatt@gmail.com.
